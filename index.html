About

Independent music cartel from Tricity, Poland.

Parasol Sound Cartel is based in the Tri-City area of Gdańsk, Sopot and Gdynia — focused on raw, live music creation and uncompromised audiovisual documentation.

Our primary goal is maintaining a direct, unfiltered connection with our recipients. We distribute exclusive content and new drops directly to our cartel members. Membership is completely free — just subscribe below.

Projects & Formats
01

Chiaroscuro Sessions

Cyclic live recording sessions. Bands play 2–3 tracks live in a single take without a safety net. What comes out is a pure document of the moment.

02

Usual Suspects

Fully improvised jam sessions driven by spontaneous energy exchange and unscripted musical dialogue.

03

Additional Drops

Standalone music releases, including the single "Bałtyk" by Artur Bieszke.
