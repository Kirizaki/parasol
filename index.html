FIRST DROP!

Chiaroscuro Sessions

We are thrilled to present a new series of live sessions inspired by the idea of chiaroscuro.

Black and white recordings, filmed in dark rooms where strong contrast of light and shadow shapes the form — both the image and the improvisation. Raw atmosphere, one lens, light and music. Each session is a meeting of sound, space, and spontaneous form.

First drop

JUTRA: Chiaroscuro Sessions

The first edition of the series opens with JUTRA. Their energy in a closed, intimate space is something you have to see. As a cartel member, you get the premiere link before everyone else.

Artist

JUTRA

Jutra is an alt-jazz quintet from Tricity, Poland, currently preparing for the release of their second album A Mid-Sized Universe.


The band's music is anchored in the vibrant contemporary jazz scene of Tricity, Poland. They draw inspiration from many aesthetics: the beat-driven, process-oriented approach of Chicago alt-jazz (Makaya McCraven, Jeff Parker), the cinematic soulfulness and expressive songwriting of the Melbourne/Naarm scene (Temporary Blessings, Surprise Chef, Karate Boogaloo), and the timeless power of ethio-jazz (Mulatu Astatke), where simple forms take on enormous expression.

The Parasol Sound Cartel is thrilled to report that our latest transmission, Usual Suspects Vol.001, has officially infiltrated Bandcamp's list of The Best Jazz of July 2026!


To see our raw, unfiltered jam session land on such a prestigious roster has left us genuinely amazed. "the smallest atom is its own energy..." — Thomas Mann


New Release

Usual Suspects Vol.002

Here's something different. This jam is a blind, snowy road winding deep through the forest. Intense, cosmic, and speeding rapidly into the dark.


This session is the sonic proof of those words: a raw explosion where every spontaneous note carries its own universe. Far from a planned composition, this gathering of Wojciech Lacki (bass), Tomasz Gołda (drums), and Maciej Szkudlarek (synths) channels a collective, spiritual force that feels almost alive.


While the cover hints at a harsh, snowy path through the dark, the audio itself is the energy illuminating that path — a trance-like, afro-rock fusion where groovy basslines and cosmic synths collide in real-time. It is a reminder that in the chaos of unplanned collaboration, the smallest rhythmic shift holds the power to transport you entirely.

Recorded in NDK M34.

Lineup

  • Wojciech Lacki — bass
  • Tomasz Gołda — drums
  • Maciej Szkudlarek — synths

About

Independent music cartel from Tricity, Poland.

Parasol Sound Cartel is based in the Tri-City area of Gdańsk, Sopot and Gdynia — focused on raw, live music creation and uncompromised audiovisual documentation.

Our primary goal is maintaining a direct, unfiltered connection with our recipients. We distribute exclusive content and new drops directly to our cartel members. Membership is completely free — just subscribe below.

Projects & Formats
01

Chiaroscuro Sessions

Cyclic live recording sessions. Bands play 2–3 tracks live in a single take without a safety net. What comes out is a pure document of the moment.

02

Usual Suspects

Fully improvised jam sessions driven by spontaneous energy exchange and unscripted musical dialogue.

03
