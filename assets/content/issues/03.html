The Best Jazz on Bandcamp, July 2026

"It's got the spirit of the jam, with all kinds of blistering heat and pulsing rhythms — straight-ahead modern jazz served up in generous portions." — Dave Sumner
Bandcamp Feature

The Parasol Sound Cartel is thrilled to report that our latest transmission, Usual Suspects Vol.001, has officially infiltrated Bandcamp's list of The Best Jazz of July 2026!


To see our raw, unfiltered jam session land on such a prestigious roster has left us genuinely amazed. While our primary mission has never been to chase awards — as our true goal remains the pure act of creation — this recognition serves as a massive motivational kick just as our journey begins. We might be a cartel, but today we're simply shock-happy musicians eager to share this victory with you. Thank you for tuning in.
