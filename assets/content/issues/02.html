Usual Suspects Vol.001

Another drop in the game, same creative spark. Usual Suspects is here for you.


This is a series of musical gatherings bringing together friends and collaborators to explore spontaneous improvisation and collective creativity. Focused on real-time sonic exploration and unplanned collaboration. Each session results in a unique, unrepeatable performance.


Recorded in NDK M34 on Revox A77.
