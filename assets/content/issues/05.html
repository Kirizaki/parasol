BAŁTYK

BAŁTYK is the first piece from an electronic endeavor executed by Artur Bieszke.


Previously known as a member of a number of Tricity bands, now bashing together soundscapes and glitches. The track was an opening of the concert that Artur played in July at Morska Szum #3 at NDK M34 before Bogdan Raczyński. Read more about Morska Szum #3 →


Dig into the aquatic breakbeats, while a larger release from Artur is upcoming later this year!
